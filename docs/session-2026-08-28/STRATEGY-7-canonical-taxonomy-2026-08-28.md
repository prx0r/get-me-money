## The three markets

```
RESOURCE MARKET          CAPABILITY MARKET         OUTCOME MARKET
raw inputs               normalized actions        finished work
    ↓                         ↓                         ↓
models, endpoints,      research, verify,          report, dataset,
compute, search,        extract, translate,        application,
datasets, workers       code, generate,            analysis, result
                        evaluate
```

---

## The five categories

```
PARTS       things used to make other things
PRODUCTS    finished reusable things
SERVICES    capabilities you invoke
WORK        jobs someone wants completed
WORKERS     economic actors composing all of the above
```
