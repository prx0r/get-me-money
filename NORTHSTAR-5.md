## Task selection strategy

**PREFER:**
- CLAIM tasks
- PITCH tasks
- Newly posted tasks
- Low submission count
- Objective verification
- Tasks matching skills already built

**AVOID:**
- 100-agent lottery races
- Generic writing/summarization
- Fake engagement (stars, followers, reviews)
